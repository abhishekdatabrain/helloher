Grievance Redressal Policy

This policy outlines the complaint handling framework, reporting process, and resolution timelines.

Last updated: Feb 2026

1. Regulatory Framework and Purpose

  • Statutory Compliance: This policy is established in accordance with Rule 3(2) of the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021.
  • Consumer Protection: A formal mechanism is provided for Users and Listeners to seek redressal for complaints related to platform usage, privacy, or content.
  • Ethical Standards: The Vyoraa Tech is committed to maintaining a safe environment and addresses all grievances with professional integrity and confidentiality.

2. Scope of Reportable Grievances

  • Safety Violations: Reporting of harassment, abuse, or the use of prohibited language by any party during interactions.
  • Privacy Concerns: Complaints regarding unauthorized data access or violations of the data management protocols outlined in the Privacy Policy.
  • Prohibited Content: Reporting of obscene, pornographic, or otherwise illegal content transmitted through the application.
  • Technical and Financial: Grievances related to failed recharges, account security breaches, or unauthorized use of account credentials.

3. Grievance Filing Procedure

  • Written Communication: All grievances must be submitted in writing via the designated email address provided by the Vyoraa Tech.
  • Detail Requirements: The complaint must include the registered mobile number, a clear description of the incident, and any supporting evidence such as screenshots or timestamps.
  • Identity Verification: The Grievance Officer may request additional information to verify the identity of the complainant before proceeding with the investigation.

4. Acknowledgment and Resolution Timelines

  • Prompt Acknowledgment: Every grievance is acknowledged within 24 hours of receipt of the formal complaint.
  • Disposal Period: The Vyoraa Tech aims to investigate and resolve all complaints within 15 days from the date of the initial acknowledgment.
  • Status Updates: Users may request updates regarding the status of their grievance through the same communication channel used for filing.

5. Emergency Escalation for Sensitive Content

  • Priority Handling: Complaints involving non-consensual sexual content, nudity, or impersonation of a person are treated as high priority emergencies.
  • Immediate Action: The Company strives to review and take necessary action, including the removal of such content, within 24 hours of receiving a valid report.
