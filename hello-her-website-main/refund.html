Refund & Cancellation Policy

These terms describe how digital credits, refunds, and service cancellations are handled on the platform.

Last updated: Feb 2026

1. General Principles of the Wallet System

  • Digital Credit Nature: Hello Her operates on a prepaid virtual wallet system where Indian Rupees (INR) or other applicable currencies are converted into in-app digital credits.
  • Non-Transferability: Once credits are successfully added to a User’s wallet, they are non-transferable to other users and hold no monetary value outside of the platform environment.
  • Usage Dependency: Credits are consumed in real time based on the duration of voice calls or the volume of text interactions as determined by the platform’s pricing engine.
  • Expiration Policy: Digital credits may be subject to expiration if an account remains inactive for a period exceeding 365 days, unless otherwise mandated by local consumer laws.
  • Wallet Integrity: Any attempt to manipulate the wallet balance through unauthorized software or fraudulent activities will result in the immediate forfeiture of the entire balance and blocking of user’s account.

2. Eligibility Criteria for Refunds

  • Technical Failure: A refund is considered only when a successful payment has been processed from the User's bank account but the corresponding credits are not reflected in the App wallet.
  • Duplicate Transactions: In cases where a system glitch results in a User being charged multiple times for a single recharge request, the duplicate amount is eligible for a refund only in case of duplicate credited into the App wallet.
  • Server-Side Disconnections: If a call is terminated within the first 30 seconds due to a verified platform-side server failure, the credits for that specific interaction may be reversed.
  • Unauthorized Access: If a transaction is proven to be the result of a security breach within the platform’s infrastructure, a manual review for refund eligibility will be initiated.
  • Legal Mandate: Refunds are processed in specific scenarios where the laws of India or the relevant jurisdiction of the User mandate a reversal of a digital transaction.

3. Non-Refundable Scenarios and Exclusions

  • User Satisfaction: Refunds are not granted based on a User’s dissatisfaction with the companionship, personality, or quality of conversation provided by a Listener.
  • Device and Network Issues: No refunds are issued for call drops or chat failures resulting from the User’s unstable internet connection, battery depletion, or hardware malfunctions.
  • Voluntary Account Deletion: If a User chooses to delete their account while a balance remains in the wallet, such balance is considered forfeited and is not eligible for a refund.
  • Guideline Violations: Accounts that are suspended or permanently banned for violating the Terms and Conditions or User Conduct rules lose all rights to any remaining wallet balance.
  • Accidental Purchases: The platform is not responsible for accidental recharges or calls initiated by the User, and such credits are deemed consumed or non-refundable.

4. Refund Request and Documentation Process

  • Reporting Timeline: All refund claims must be formally submitted to the Vyoraa Tech within 48 hours of the disputed transaction.
  • Mandatory Evidence: A claim must include the unique Transaction ID, the mobile number registered with the App, and a valid screenshot of the bank or wallet payment confirmation.
  • Submission Channel: Requests are only accepted through the official in-app support module or by emailing the designated support address at support@helloher.in
  • Review Procedure: Each request undergoes a manual audit of the platform’s server logs and payment gateway reports to verify the validity of the claim.
  • Communication: Users are notified of the approval or rejection of their refund request via their registered email address or in-app notification system.

5. Processing Timelines and Payout Methods

  • Verification Period: The internal investigation of a refund claim is typically completed within 3 to 5 business days from the date of receipt of all required documents.
  • Payout Source: Approved refunds are processed back to the original source of payment (Bank Account, Credit Card, or UPI) used during the initial transaction.
  • Bank Processing Time: Once the Company initiates a refund, the actual credit to the User's account may take between 7 to 10 business days, depending on the banking institution’s policies.
  • Service Fee Deductions: The Company reserves the right to deduct any third-party payment gateway processing fees or applicable taxes from the final refund amount.

6. Cancellation of Membership Services

  • User-Initiated Cancellation: Users may stop using the Membership Services at any time by ceasing recharges or removing the application from their device.
  • Effect of Cancellation: Cancellation of a recurring subscription or membership does not entitle the User to a refund for the period already utilized or the current billing cycle.
  • Service Discontinuation: Vyoraa Tech reserves the right to modify or discontinue any specific feature or Membership Service without prior notice or liability for refunds.
  • Survival of Terms: The obligations of the User regarding payments made prior to cancellation remain in full force and effect until all financial reconciliations are complete.

7. Dispute Resolution and Jurisdiction

  • Harmonious Interpretation: This policy is to be read in conjunction with the User Terms and Conditions; in case of conflict, the Terms and Conditions shall prevail.
  • Final Authority: The decision of Vyoraa Tech regarding the eligibility of a refund based on server logs and technical evidence shall be final and binding.
  • Legal Recourse: Any unresolved disputes related to refunds shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts located in the city of Bhopal where the Vyoraa Tech's registered office in India.
